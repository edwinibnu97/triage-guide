# Privacy Policy — Triage Guide for Jira

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Triage Guide for Jira ("the app") is a Forge app by Edwin Ibnu. It helps Jira users triage work items by recommending a priority, an assignee, and labels.

## Data the app processes

- The app reads Jira work-item data (summary, description, status, priority, labels, assignee, reporter, comments) and Teamwork Graph data (teams, team members, assigned work items, goals) **only for the work item you ask it to triage**, and only through Atlassian's own APIs, acting as you (the signed-in user).
- The app **does not store any personal data**. It has no database and no storage. Recommendations are computed on the fly and shown to you; nothing is saved by the app.
- The app **does not send your data to any third party**. It makes no network calls outside Atlassian's APIs.
- The app does **not use tracking, analytics, or advertising**.
